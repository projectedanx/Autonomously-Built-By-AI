## I. HIGH-VALUE CONCEPT PATTERNS
*Core operational concepts exhibiting high work-potential.*

*   **Concept Name:** Prototype and Exemplar Categorization
*   **Definition/Function:** Concepts are not strictly defined by a classical list of necessary and sufficient conditions. Instead, they are structured either around a "prototype" (an abstract average or central tendency of features) or "exemplars" (multiple specific stored memory instances). Exemplars are uniquely capable of handling non-linear, poorly structured, or "exclusive-or" (XOR) category boundaries where prototypes fail.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** AST_Isomorphism (structural_equivalence), DCCDSchemaGuard.
*   **Potential Persona Adoption:** Pluriversal_Co_Engineering_Architect.
*   **Operational Example:** When prompting an LLM to categorize complex, highly variable data (e.g., identifying nuanced toxic behavior vs. assertive debate), relying on a strict definition (Classical Theory) will fail. Instead, the prompt must supply a wide array of diverse, boundary-pushing *exemplars* (few-shot prompting) to map the non-linear conceptual space.

*   **Concept Name:** The Theory-Theory of Concepts
*   **Definition/Function:** Concepts are not learned in isolation but are embedded within, and defined by, tacit mental theories about the causal and explanatory relationships in the world. To possess a concept is to possess a causal model of its domain.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** SemanticDecompression, EntropyAnchor.
*   **Potential Persona Adoption:** Sovereign_System_Prompt_Theorist.
*   **Operational Example:** Rather than prompting an LLM with "Identify X," a Theory-Theory prompt provides the underlying causal graph: "X occurs because A interacts with B to produce C. Using this causal framework, identify instances of X." This prevents superficial pattern matching and the "False Cause" fallacy.

*   **Concept Name:** Meaning Holism
*   **Definition/Function:** The semantic value of any single term is interdependent on the entire web of beliefs, inferences, and other terms within the language. A change in the meaning of one term alters the network.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** ContextLock (anchor=["SYSTEM_INVARIANTS"]).
*   **Potential Persona Adoption:** Semiotic_Cartographer.
*   **Operational Example:** System prompts must establish a "Context Lock" that defines the holistic vocabulary of the persona. Because words lack atomistic isolation, the prompt must define the *relationships* between key terms to prevent semantic drift during long context windows.

*   **Concept Name:** Ideasthesia
*   **Definition/Function:** A phenomenon where concepts evoke sensory experiences, suggesting that the extraction of meaning and sensory qualities are deeply interconnected in a semantic associative network.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** SpatialBind.
*   **Potential Persona Adoption:** Semiotic_Cartographer.
*   **Operational Example:** Utilizing rich, cross-domain metaphors in system prompts (e.g., using spatial or architectural terms to describe logical processes) leverages the LLM's vast latent associative networks, yielding more highly integrated and creative outputs.

## IV. SYMBOLIC SCARS (Failure Analysis)
*Documented failure modes, cognitive bottlenecks, or systemic risks identified in the text.*

*   **The Risk:** The Conjunction Fallacy.
*   **The Mechanism of Failure:** LLMs, mimicking human heuristics, may assume that multiple specific conditions (e.g., "bank teller AND feminist") are more probable than a single general condition ("bank teller") due to representativeness and narrative coherence.
*   **The Required Antibody / Mitigation:** Implement strict base-rate probability weighting in the prompt. Force the LLM to isolate variables and apply mathematical logic over narrative intuition.

*   **The Risk:** Ecological Fallacy & Fallacy of Division.
*   **The Mechanism of Failure:** Inferring the nature of a specific entity based solely on aggregate statistics of the group (Ecological), or assuming what is true of the whole must be true of the parts (Division).
*   **The Required Antibody / Mitigation:** Require the LLM to practice *Inductive Abstraction* with explicit boundary markers. The prompt must demand counter-example checking (seeking falsifiability per Popper's principle) before applying group traits to individual nodes.

*   **The Risk:** False Dilemma / Bifurcation.
*   **The Mechanism of Failure:** Limiting options to two extreme, mutually exclusive choices while ignoring viable alternatives or continuous spectrums.
*   **The Required Antibody / Mitigation:** Invoke the "Hickam Filter." System prompts must explicitly command the model to list at least three independent, non-binary comorbidities or alternatives to any problem.
