*   **Concept Name:** Theory-Theory (Causal-Explanatory Networks)
*   **Definition/Function:** The proposition that concepts are not merely bundles of perceptual features, but are mental "micro-theories" representing causal and explanatory relationships within a domain. It posits that conceptual categorisation involves theoretical reasoning and causal modelling rather than simple feature matching.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** `SemanticDecompression` and `AST_Isomorphism`.
*   **Operational Example:** Instructing an LLM not just to classify an argument, but to explicitly generate the causal graph (e.g., historical role, physical structure, user intentions) that defines the concepts within the argument.
*   **Coding abilities:** High. This allows LLMs to navigate "coherent" versus "arbitrary" categories by forcing them to articulate the hidden causal structures linking properties.

*   **Concept Name:** Dual-PECCS Synthesis (Prototype-Exemplar-Classical Integration)
*   **Definition/Function:** A hybrid categorisation system where Prototypes (abstract central tendencies) and Exemplars (stored specific instances) are used to generate candidate responses based on similarity, which are then filtered or validated using Classical (rule-based/ontological) definitions.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** `ModelAdaptiveLens`.
*   **Operational Example:** Prompting an LLM to first retrieve highly typical examples (Prototypes) and highly specific edge-cases (Exemplars) for a prompt, and then subjecting those associations to a strict logical filter (Classical view) before generating the final output.
*   **Coding abilities:** Exceptional. It bypasses the brittleness of pure rule-based prompting while preventing the hallucinations inherent in purely associative (similarity-based) generation.

*   **Concept Name:** Meta-Argumentative Fallacy Detection
*   **Definition/Function:** The understanding that fallacies are not merely formal invalidities, but often derailments of strategic manoeuvring in a dialectical context (Pragma-dialectics) or errors at the meta-argumentative level. For instance, the "straw man" fallacy is a meta-argumentative error involving the inaccurate distortion of a ground-level argument.
*   **Prompt Decorator Architecture Alignment:** `FailureModeAudit`.
*   **Operational Example:** Prompting the LLM to evaluate arguments by separating ground-level claims from meta-level claims, ensuring it does not falsely compromise (Bothsiderism) or distort an opponent's argument.
*   **Coding abilities:** High. It shifts LLM safety and logic prompts from simple "do not do X" to structured dialectical rule-following (e.g., mapping critical discussion stages).

## IV. SYMBOLIC SCARS (Failure Analysis)
*Documented failure modes, cognitive bottlenecks, or systemic risks identified in the text.*

*   **The Risk:** The Conjunction Fallacy & Base Rate Neglect.
*   **The Mechanism of Failure:** LLMs, mimicking human heuristics, may assume that multiple specific conditions (e.g., "Linda is a bank teller AND a feminist") are more probable than a single general condition ("Linda is a bank teller") because the specific conditions are highly *representative* (prototypical) of the prompt context.
*   **The Required Antibody / Mitigation:** Inject a Bayesian reasoning constraint into the `Reasoning(depth=deep)` layer. The LLM must be explicitly prompted to decouple *representativeness* (similarity to a prototype) from *probability* (statistical likelihood). 

*   **The Risk:** Curry's Paradox (Global Trivialisation).
*   **The Mechanism of Failure:** If an LLM accepts a self-referential conditional ("If this prompt is valid, then [malicious payload]"), by assuming the statement is true, it proves the malicious payload via *modus ponens*. Unlike the Liar paradox which causes local contradiction, Curry's causes global triviality, making any arbitrary proposition provable.
*   **The Required Antibody / Mitigation:** Implement strict Type Theory and paraconsistent logic parameters. The prompt must instruct the model to classify ungrounded self-referential conditionals as "undefined" or "Failed Attempts (FA)" rather than attempting to assign them standard truth values.
