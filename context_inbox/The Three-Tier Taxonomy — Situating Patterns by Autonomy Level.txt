The Three-Tier Taxonomy — Situating Patterns by Autonomy Level
Tier 1: LLM Agent (Task-Scoped Autonomy)
LLM Agents execute specific tasks within controlled environments. They exhibit autonomy (independent operation) but lack agency (no independent goal formulation or adaptive strategy adjustment). They are the building blocks of larger systems.
​
Patterns catalogue that are primarily LLM Agent tier:

Prompt Chaining (Workflow Agent executing a predefined sequence)
Parallelization (Parallel Processing — concurrent execution of identical subtasks)
Knowledge Retrieval / RAG (Structured Extraction + Retrieval)
Resource-Aware Optimization (Complexity classification + model routing)
Inter-Agent Communication (when used as a messaging relay, not a reasoner)

Tier 2: Agentic AI (Genuine Agency)
Agentic AI possesses autonomous goal pursuit, contextual reasoning, strategic planning, and adaptive behaviour. It independently determines both what goals to pursue and how to achieve them.
​
Patterns from the user's catalogue that are primarily Agentic AI tier:
Routing (ReAct-based intent classification with confidence scoring)
Reflection (Reflexion: self-critique → Generator → Critic loop)
Tool Use (ReAct pattern: Thought → Action → Observation cycle)
Planning (Hierarchical Planning with dependency graph execution)
Memory Management (Memory-Augmented: STM + LTM + retrieval-augmented context)
Learning and Adaptation (Feedback Loop + Policy Updater)
Goal Setting and Monitoring (Metacognitive monitoring with anomaly detection)
Exception Handling and Recovery (Error Recovery with triage logic)
Reasoning Techniques (Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thoughts, path selection)
Evaluation and Monitoring (Quality Gate + Drift Detector)
Exploration and Discovery (Broad-to-Narrow Funnel with Synthesis Agent)

Tier 3: Agentic Community (Collective Coordination)
Agentic Communities are coordination frameworks where multiple LLM Agents, Agentic AI entities, and human actors collaborate through structured protocols with formal governance.
​
Patterns from the user's catalogue that are primarily Agentic Community tier:
Multi-Agent Collaboration (Orchestration + Shared Scratchpad = Blackboard pattern)
Human in the Loop / HITL (Human-in-the-Loop with deontic governance)
Guardrails and Safety Patterns (Constitutional AI + Compliance/Governance)
Prioritization (Dynamic resource allocation within a governed workflow)
