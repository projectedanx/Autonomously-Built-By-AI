### The Four Pillars of Specification Planning

#### 1. Automated Discovery and Constraint Mining (The Context of Out-Manoeuvring)
To systematically trigger creative abduction, the system must recognize when current theoretical frameworks are under-determined by the environment and actively induce an attitude of "preparedness to abandon old convictions and seek new ones". 

*   **Hard Invariants (The Out-Manoeuvring Constraint):** The consciously working mind, relying on rigid logical rules, must be programmatically prevented from locking the system into repetitive, tautological deduction or rote, mechanical applications of existing theory (the "cookie-cutter" trap).
*   **Soft Targets (Attitude Parameterization):** The harness must optimize for a state where "data are to be taken seriously, and the validity of previously developed knowledge is to be queried".

#### 2. Isomorphic Formalization (From Epistemic Triggers to State Transitions)

##### A. The Detective Macro-Strategy (High-Pressure Forcing)
This pathway is triggered by a self-induced emergency state, characterized by "genuine doubt, uncertainty, fear, or great pressure to act". Under this acute existential strain—exemplified by Peirce’s panic upon losing his watch and facing "life-long professional disgrace"—the system is forced into a state of alarm. 
*   **Logical Formalization:**
    $$\text{Let } P_{\text{cognitive}} \to \infty \quad \text{and} \quad T_{\text{available}} \to 0 \quad (\text{Acute clock-cycle starvation})$$
    $$\text{Operate: } \text{Bypass sequential verification layers }.$$
    $$\text{Action: } \text{Forced selection of an intuitive, unassured "best guess" to resolve the crisis }.$$
This process "allows no time for the calculating mind to busy itself" with rules, forcing the "abductive lightning" to strike.

##### B. The Musement Macro-Strategy (Low-Pressure Stochastic Wandering)
Conversely, the Musement pathway operates under conditions of absolute "leisure," defined as complete freedom from immediate pressure to act. 
*   **Logical Formalization:**
    $$\text{Let } P_{\text{cognitive}} \to 0 \quad \text{and} \quad T_{\text{available}} \to \infty \quad (\text{Unbounded temporal horizon})$$
    $$\text{Operate: } \text{"Skiff of musement" } \text{ — a rule-free mental game of meditation and day-dreaming }.$$
    $$\text{Action: } \text{Unconstrained cross-domain mapping using multi-modal representations (diagrams and experiments) }.$$
This process "switches off" the calculating mind's power of logical judgment, allowing the system to wander stochastically through an infinite lattice of theories.

#### 4. Continuous Falsification and Edge-Case Stress Testing

To verify the dual-pathway harness before active runtime deployment, we execute simulated edge-case failures:

1.  **Novice bottom-up tinkering (Detective Path Failure):**
    *   *Failure Mode:* Under high pressure, the system fails to apply top-down strategic reasoning and instead falls back on undirected, random physical manipulations of resources without an overarching hypothesis (the novice makerspace failure mode).
    *   *Mitigation:* Programmatically enforce **Schoenfeld’s Metacognitive Check**. The system must inter-personally or intra-personally self-regulate by generating multiple top-down system hypotheses and "ruthlessly" rejecting unworkable paths.
2.  **Atheoretical Drift and Wild Gooses (Musement Path Failure):**
    *   *Failure Mode:* Free-floating musement generates an infinite array of highly ungrounded, "grotesque" blends that are physically or logically absurd.
    *   *Mitigation:* Introduce the **Pragmatic Maxim Gate**. Any candidate hypothesis generated during rule-free play must be immediately evaluated for "conceivable practical bearings" and "experimental verifiability". If it fails to yield testable implications, it is pruned from the active queue.
