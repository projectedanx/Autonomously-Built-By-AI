### Deconstruction of AST Severity Levels and Corrective Actions

#### Level 1: Warning (Transient Anomaly Gate)
*   **Topological/Geometric Signature:** A **transient Symbolic Scar** with low topological persistence; a minor curvature dip of approximately **$-5\%$** in the local manifold geometry.
*   **RTA Corrective Action:** The system logs the event to the `REPAIR.cxep.log` and issues a localized warning warning to the implicated agents, notifying them of potential **confidence model drift**.
*   **Systems Engineering Mechanics:** This serves as an early-warning signal. It introduces zero operational latency or runtime friction, allowing the system to maintain its **Cost of Structural Discovery (CSD)** and explore novel pathways without premature execution gating.

#### Level 2: Recalibration (Localized Calibration Shift)
*   **Topological/Geometric Signature:** A **moderately persistent Symbolic Scar** ($\beta_1$ loop); a significant local curvature dip of approximately **$-20\%$**.
*   **RTA Corrective Action:** The RTA triggers a **targeted confidence recalibration** (e.g., temperature scaling) for the implicated agents.
*   **Systems Engineering Mechanics:** The system dynamically adjusts the model's logits to shape-shift its probability distribution. By increasing the temperature or applying localized calibration parameters, the agent is forced to **verbalize higher uncertainty** in its predictions, dampening the threat of overconfident hallucinations (Confidence-Fidelity Divergence).

#### Level 3: Re-weighting (Structural Demotion)
*   **Topological/Geometric Signature:** A **highly persistent Symbolic Scar**; a major local curvature collapse of approximately **$-50\%$**.
*   **RTA Corrective Action:** The RTA **reduces the voting weight** of the implicated agents in the ensemble prediction and flags the spurious features for down-weighting.
*   **Systems Engineering Mechanics:** This acts as a structural defense-in-depth barrier. While the compromised agents continue to compute, their "cognitive authority" within the collective consensus mechanism is systematically depreciated. This contains the blast radius of the compromised logic, ensuring that the global consensus trajectory remains anchored to the non-negotiable fixed points of the **Semantic Genome Architecture (SGA)**.

#### Level 4: Quarantine (Epistemic Escrow & Circuit Breaking)
*   **Topological/Geometric Signature:** A **critical, expanding Symbolic Scar**; total manifold fragmentation.
*   **RTA Corrective Action:** The RTA **halts execution**, places the task and its context in **Epistemic Escrow**, **quarantines** the implicated agents from the active ensemble, and escalates the system state to **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) oversight**.
*   **Systems Engineering Mechanics:** When the system’s calculated **Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index (CFDI)** breaches the maximum safety threshold ($CFDI > 0.1$), the **Epistemic Escrow** circuit breaker is tripped.

    3.  **Escalation:** The system generates a **Justified Uncertainty Report (JUR)**. Autonomous execution remains locked in this **Positive Friction Layer (PFL)** until a human operator validates the data, adjusts the rules, or authorizes an **Algorithmic Reparation** sequence.
