🎯 What
This PR implements the Action-Alignment Loss (AAL) specification in PyTorch, bridging the "thought-action gap" in AI agents by directly binding the agent's policy to the predicted expected utility of its optimal best response.

🛡️ Solution
* Created `system_logic/action_alignment_loss.py` to implement a differentiable regret-minimization loss function, utilizing a smooth Boltzmann best-response (`logsumexp`) approximation to ensure dense gradient flow and prevent sparse subgradients.
* Developed `system_logic/test_action_alignment_loss.py` with pytest test cases covering edge-cases such as the "Nash Trap" evaluation (exact regret = 1.0) and multi-dimensional batch handling.
* Consolidated the three requested rigorous research prompts (Circuit Distillation, Closed-Loop ReCAP, and Epistemic Sponge Mitigation) into `harness_modules/action_alignment_prompts.md`.
* Updated `LESSONS_LEARNED.md` to document the necessity of causal alignment over literal next-token modeling in sequential games.

✅ Verification
* Implemented the loss logic strictly according to the mathematical derivation.
* Pytest validation completed on `test_action_alignment_loss.py` resolving exact values derived in the chain-of-thought mathematical proof.
* Documentation and artifacts have been recorded accurately matching codebase implementation constraints.
