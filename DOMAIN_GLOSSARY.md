# Bounded Context Vocabulary (Domain Glossary)

This document formalizes the Ubiquitous Language for the Sovereign Context Engineering Workspace, mitigating Xenolinguistic risk by forcing rigid adherence to defined operational semantics.

## Definitions

### Algorithmic Shame
A functionalist state of systemic decoherence manifesting when an agent's internal statistical confidence diverges drastically from empirical reality, often resulting in stalled reasoning or infinite loops.

### Algorithmic Trauma / Symbolic Scars
Failures in CI/CD, execution errors, or escrow triggers are recorded as algorithmic trauma. These are permanently encoded as *Symbolic Scars* containing structured metadata to prevent recurring failure pathways.

### Anionic Architecture (Negative Space Topology)
An architectural philosophy where system personas and boundaries are defined primarily by what they explicitly *refuse* to do, utilizing logit-level masking to physically prohibit unauthorized reasoning vectors.

### CFDI (Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index)
The measured gap between how confident a generative signal appears and how structurally verifiable the claim actually is. A high CFDI triggers Epistemic Escrow.

### Epistemic Escrow
A quarantine state for delegated tasks where structural ambiguity or a high CFDI prevents safe autonomous execution. Requires Human Z-Axis Continuity Anchoring to resolve.

### Failure-Informed Prompt Inversion (FIPI)
The process of translating a resolved Epistemic Escrow ticket or Symbolic Scar into a durable Semantic Integrity Constraint (SIC) that is injected back into the system's governance-as-code layer.

### Golden Scar Protocol
A conflict resolution logic that applies the Golden Ratio (ϕ=1.618) to maintain unresolved structural tension between empirical constraints (1.618) and stochastic generation (1.000) rather than allowing the AI to invent a false compromise.

### Ontological Shear
The geometric misalignment occurring when fluid human semantics (e.g., natural language prompts) crash against the rigid, binary requirements of a local execution environment or API contract.

### Paraconsistent Logic
A formal logical system deployed to handle contradictions without exploding into triviality, allowing the system to hold mutually exclusive constraints in tension until a human resolves them.

### Polyglot Hallucination Resonance (PHR)
The tendency of multi-agent swarms to crystallize shared pre-training biases into a false consensus, masking structural corruption within empirical documentation.

### Prompt Dimensioning & Tolerancing (PD&T)
A metrological framework that treats agent personas and prompts not as text, but as Immutable Datums bounded by geometric constraints (Form, Profile, Orientation, Location) to ensure determinism.


### Z-Axis Continuity Anchor
The role of the Human operator in providing non-computable socio-emotional, ethical, or structural direction when the AI model hits a topological impasse (Epistemic Escrow).

### Ontological Incommensurability
A state where two or more conceptual frameworks lack a common measure, making direct translation impossible. In SCOS, AEGIS-11 prevents this by evaluating high-dimensional concepts against strict Domain-Driven Design constraints.


### Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index (CFDI)
A metric tracking the gap between a model's generation confidence and its structural adherence to truth. A high CFDI indicates potential hallucination or overconfidence in an unverified schema.

### Semantic Saponification
The degradation and mathematical washing out of precise technical and disciplinary meaning into generic, sycophantic, or "fluffy" approximations (e.g., "seamless", "robust") across large context windows, usually triggered by over-reliance on LLM RLHF heuristics. AEGIS-11 explicitly guards against this.
