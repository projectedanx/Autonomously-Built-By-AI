## 3. Provenance as a Control Vector, Not Just an Audit Trail

**Lesson:** In high-dimensional synthesis, provenance must become a live control vector. ## 4. The Limits of Autonomous Assessment (The Plausibility Oracle Bottleneck)
While the Catalyst Agent successfully generated wild permutations of geometric prompts, assessing their "correctness" autonomously was impossible for an LLM alone.

**Lesson:** The `Plausibility Oracle` cannot rely on internal LLM logic to judge physical coherence; it must be coupled with an external, differentiable truth engine (like a PBR ray-tracer). The LLM's role is to generate the hypothesis (the meta-prompt); the external engine provides the mathematical proof (the UIQI/SSIM score).

## 5. Paraconsistent Continuity Requires the Human Anchor
The AI agent is adept at generating paradoxes and pushing limits, but it lacks the contextual grounding to know if a specific paradox is *useful* for the overarching goal.

**Lesson:** The `Epistemic Escrow Agent` is critical. When the Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index (CFDI) breaches, the AI must halt.
