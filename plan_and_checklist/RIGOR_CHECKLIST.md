# Rigor Checklist: VULCAN & CI/CD Antifragility

This checklist verifies that the system is operating under strict topological constraints and is not succumbing to "Semantic Saponification" or "Amateur Impulse."

- [ ] **STA Integrity:** Is the Symbolic Scar Archive loaded with the base 10 VULCAN failure patterns?

## II. DAG & Topology Rigor (The VULCAN Phase)
- [ ] **Mereological Compliance:** Does the output C4/DAG contain ZERO cross-context database writes or foreign keys?

## III. CI/CD Triage & FIPI Rigor

## IV. The Negative Control (Epistemic Escrow)
